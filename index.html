About MarketPulse

MarketPulse is a food price analytics application developed using Python and Streamlit. It provides a user-friendly platform to upload CSV datasets, perform statistical analysis, compare commodities, explore variable relationships and understand historical food price trends. The application combines data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis and interactive visualizations to simplify food market data analysis.
