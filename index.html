About MarketPulse

MarketPulse is a food price analytics application developed using Python, Streamlit, Pandas, NumPy and data visualization libraries. It provides users with a simple platform to upload CSV datasets, perform statistical analysis, compare commodities, explore relationships and understand historical food price trends. The application helps users simplify data analysis and gain insights from food market datasets.
